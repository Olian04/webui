# Glossary

The words this handbook uses, and where they are in the application.

| Word | Meaning | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Alert** | Raised when a device stops reporting | [Alerts](/alert) |
| **Collector** | The service that gathers events from a site's devices | [Config](/config) and [System](/system) |
| **Device** | Something that reports to a collector | [Devices](/device) |
| **Queue depth** | How many events wait for a [sink](/handbook/sinks); above 5 000 it is not keeping up | [System](/system) |
| **Sample** | The share of events that is kept | [Config](/config) |
| **Site** | Where devices are, such as [Stockholm](/site/Stockholm) or [Göteborg](/site/G%C3%B6teborg) | [Sites](/site) |
| **Sink** | Where the collector sends what it has gathered | [Sinks](/handbook/sinks) |
